## Our position — the sweet middle

We sell **onshore-quality output at nearshore price**, which is exactly what AI-native delivery makes possible. That is the argument, and this is the card:

| Role | Rate/hr | Used for |
|---|---:|---|
| Product manager / discovery | **$85** | Intake, PRD, stakeholder sessions, UAT coordination |
| Senior AI-native engineer | **$75** | All build work, backend and frontend |
| Product designer | **$70** | Design system, page specs, wireframes, prototypes |
| QA / accessibility / security review | **$60** | Test passes, audits, remediation verification |
| **Blended default** | **$70** | The single rate used on fixed-price quotations |

**$70/hr blended** is the default. It sits above every offshore shop the client will compare against — which is the point, because it signals we are not one — and roughly 45% below a Western European agency. It is defensible in any negotiation: it is the median of the global professional market, not the median of the marketplace race to the bottom.

### When to move off $70

| Situation | Rate | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| Client is a funded startup in US/UK/DACH | $85–95 | They benchmark against local agencies, not Upwork |
| Client is a pre-revenue founder, personal budget | $55–62 | Only with reduced scope, never with reduced quality gates |
| Retainer, ≥60 h/month committed, 3-month minimum | $62 | Volume discount, stated as such |
| Regulated domain (health, fintech, gov) | $90–110 | Compliance, audit trail, and liability are real cost |
| Rush: compressed calendar requiring weekend work | +25% | On the affected phase only, itemised |
| Fixed price with unsigned requirements | +15% | Or refuse and sell a discovery phase instead |

Never discount the rate to win. Discount the **scope**. A cheaper rate devalues every future quote to that client; a smaller phase one does not.

## Fixed-price construction

```
phase price = phase billable hours × blended rate, rounded to the nearest $50
project total = Σ phase prices
```

Round the total to a clean number **downward** — $23,850 becomes $23,500. It reads as considered rather than computed, and the goodwill costs less than a negotiation round.

### Payment schedule (default)

| Trigger | Share |
|---|---:|
| Signature — mobilisation | 30% |
| Midpoint milestone accepted | 40% |
| Final acceptance, before production handover | 30% |

For projects over $35k, split the middle into two 20% milestones. For a first-time client under $8k, use 50/50. Never start work on 0% up front.

### Always in the quotation

- **Validity**: 30 days from the issue date.
- **Change requests**: billed at the blended rate, minimum 2 hours, quoted and approved in writing before work starts.
- **What is excluded**, named explicitly: third-party licences and subscriptions, domain and hosting fees, paid stock assets, content writing and translation, app store developer accounts, ongoing maintenance, and anything in `docs/SCOPE.md` under Out of Scope.
- **What the client owes us**: content, brand assets, access credentials, and named decision-makers — each with the date it is needed. Client delay is the single most common cause of a slipped fixed-price project; putting the dependency in writing is what makes a later timeline conversation survivable.
- **Post-launch**: 30 days of defect fixes included (a defect is a deviation from the PRD, not a new idea). Optional care plan quoted separately at 10–15% of project value per year.
- **IP**: transfers on final payment.
